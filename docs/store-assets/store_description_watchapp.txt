YAMAKAGE - Terrain-Aware Sunrise & Sunset Predictor

■ Overview
Darkness falls much faster in the mountains than in flat areas due to terrain obstructions.
YAMAKAGE is a watch application that analyzes surrounding terrain (elevation data) to calculate and visualize "true" sunrise and sunset times, accounting for the actual mountains and shadows around you.

■ Key Features
YAMAKAGE scans terrain data within a 30km radius of your current location and cross-references it with the sun's trajectory to predict exactly when the sun will disappear behind or emerge from the terrain.
Evolving from our popular data field version, this standalone app offers enhanced visual insights, allowing you to grasp your surroundings at a glance. It is perfect for trail running, mountaineering, and cycling when accurate light management is crucial.

■ Data & Features
Launch the app and press the "START" button to execute calculations. You can then navigate through three distinct views:

・ Panorama View: Displays the elevation profile of the terrain (mountains) in the direction you are currently facing, overlaid with the sun's trajectory and your current solar position.
・ SkyPlot: Provides a 360-degree top-down view of the terrain elevations and the sun’s path across the sky.
・ Radar View: Intuitively visualizes surrounding obstacles and open areas from a top-down, radar-like perspective. It also displays the real-time distance (in km) to the obstacle in the direction your watch is facing.
・ Details View: Shows precise sunrise and sunset times adjusted for terrain, along with the elevation angle of the terrain in your current direction.

■ How it works
To minimize battery consumption and processing load on your device, YAMAKAGE performs complex terrain analysis on a dedicated cloud server.

1. The app sends your current GPS coordinates to our secure server.
2. The server instantly analyzes global elevation data within a 30km radius of your location.
3. The results—the terrain profile combined with solar trajectory data—are sent back to your device, where the app renders the graphs.

Privacy Note: While this app transmits your location to our server for calculation, we do not collect or store any personal information, including account details or device identifiers. To maintain anonymity, we use a randomly generated temporary hash for communication. Transmitted location data is never permanently stored and is discarded immediately after the calculation is complete.

■ Important Notes

・ Smartphone Connection Required: As the app fetches terrain data from the cloud, your device must be connected to your smartphone (via Garmin Connect Mobile) during the calculation process.
GPS Acquisition: The app uses your current GPS location. Please execute the calculation outdoors where a strong GPS signal is available.
Communication Failures: If the app fails to connect to the server or your smartphone, an error screen will appear. Please ensure your connection and try again.
Exceptions: In regions like Northern Europe during polar day/night, where sunrise/sunset concepts do not apply, times will be displayed as "--:--" and sunrise/sunset icons will not appear.

■ Disclaimer & Accuracy
The times and graphs provided are predictions based on elevation data (approx. 38m resolution) and solar trajectory calculations. They do not account for atmospheric conditions, surrounding vegetation, buildings, or weather (clouds), and may differ from your actual experience by several to over ten minutes.
Do not rely solely on this data for safety. Always maintain a flexible schedule and prioritize your safety in the mountains.
